I love reading theories of deep self-development and consciousness, but it can get abstract very quick.

It feels good to know more. To feel like you’re becoming smarter. But are you actually integrating those into your life?

That’s what this prompt is for.

So, if you want to become more aware, less reactive, more emotionally intelligent, or become a holistic thinker to improve your business in a meaningful way, this is for you.

First, it will ask you clarifying questions to learn about your life.

Then, it will spit out a 90-day action plan.

You can either stop there and implement it yourself, or, the AI will enter phase 2 and act as a daily accountability coach. It will guide you through each action and provide education where necessary.

That’s an incredible way to learn.
