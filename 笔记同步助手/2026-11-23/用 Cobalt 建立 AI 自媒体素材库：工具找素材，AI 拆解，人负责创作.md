## 核心发现：高效创作的关键不是苦想选题，而是建立素材库

很多人做 AI 自媒体时，每天都在为选题发愁。实际上，真正高效的人往往并不缺乏灵感，而是拥有一套持续收集、分析和再利用公开内容的流程。

Many people struggle with topic ideas when creating AI-related content. In reality, successful creators are not necessarily short on inspiration; they rely on a repeatable system for collecting, analyzing, and reusing public content.

## 开源下载工具：Cobalt

G哥发现了一款名为 Cobalt 的开源工具。截至视频发布时，该项目在 GitHub 上获得了 46.4K 个 Star。Cobalt 可以直接下载 YouTube、TikTok、X、Instagram 和哔哩哔哩等平台上的公开视频。

G哥 discovered an open-source tool called Cobalt. At the time of publication, the project had received 46.4K stars on GitHub. Cobalt can directly download public videos from YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram, and Bilibili.

## 无需付费，也无需注册会员

Cobalt 完全开源，不收取下载费用，也不需要注册付费会员，因此适合作为个人自媒体工作流中的素材采集工具。

Cobalt is fully open source and does not require a paid subscription or membership registration, making it suitable for collecting materials in a personal content-creation workflow.

## 建立自己的爆款素材库

看到值得研究的爆款内容后，可以先将其快速收集并保存，形成个人素材库。这样既能减少每天凭空寻找选题的时间，也便于持续观察热门内容的方向与表达方式。

After finding popular content worth studying, creators can collect and save it quickly to build a personal library. This reduces the time spent searching for topics from scratch and makes it easier to track popular content trends and presentation styles.

## 让 AI 拆解爆款逻辑

素材收集完成后，可以交给 AI 分析内容的选题方向、开头设计、脚本结构、镜头安排以及获得传播的原因。AI 负责提取规律和辅助拆解，创作者则结合自身定位进行重新创作，而不是简单照搬原内容。

Once the materials have been collected, AI can help analyze the topic direction, opening, script structure, shot arrangement, and the factors behind the content's popularity. AI handles pattern extraction and breakdown, while the creator develops new content according to their own positioning rather than simply copying the original.

## 高效分工：工具找素材，AI 负责分析，人负责创造

真正值得借鉴的不是某个固定选题，而是这套分工方式：工具负责快速寻找和下载素材，AI 负责归纳内容规律，人负责判断、取舍和创造。对于从事 AI 自媒体的人，这套流程可以显著降低选题与前期研究成本。

The most valuable takeaway is not a specific topic, but the division of labor: tools find and download materials, AI analyzes patterns, and people make the creative decisions. For AI content creators, this workflow can significantly reduce the cost of topic selection and preliminary research.
